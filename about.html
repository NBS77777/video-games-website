About Minecraft

Minecraft is a critically acclaimed sandbox video game developed by Mojang Studios and originally created by Markus "Notch" Persson. First released in 2011, it has become the best-selling video game in history, with hundreds of millions of copies sold across multiple platforms.

  1. 2009 — Minecraft development began
  2. 2011 — Official release
  3. 2014 — Microsoft acquired Mojang
  4. 2021 — Minecraft celebrated its 10th anniversary
  • Minecraft is a sandbox game.
  • Players can build structures.
  • The game has Survival and Creative modes.
  • Minecraft has multiplayer features.
"Minecraft is a game about placing blocks and going on adventures."— Mojang Studios

Minecraft gives players creative freedom to build and explore.
